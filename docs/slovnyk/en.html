Glossary of terms

Plain explanations of words you will meet in the courses. If a lesson uses a word you don’t know — look it up here.

39 terms
A

ACT — Acceptance and Commitment Therapy

An approach in psychotherapy. It teaches you not to fight difficult thoughts and feelings but to accept them and act in line with your values.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Anchor breathing

Slow belly breathing (in 4 · pause 1 · out 6) that steadies you, the way an anchor steadies a boat, when a wave of anxiety comes.

Used in: 01 · Тиша всередині

Assertiveness

The ability to speak about your needs, opinions and boundaries calmly and respectfully — without pushing others and without giving yourself up. Simply put: confident, not aggressive.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Association amulet

A word, gesture or object you have deliberately linked to a vivid memory of calm and confidence. Recalling it helps you return to that state faster.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Associative thinking

The brain links events, things and feelings: one part of an experience brings back the whole. One song — and you are back in that summer.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Avoidance

Steering clear of what scares us. It brings relief now, but over time the fear grows and life gets smaller.

Used in: 01 · Тиша всередині02 · Тихо в голові03 · Сміливо бути собою
B

Behavioural experiment

A way to test a fear through action: predict what will happen, take a small safe step, and compare it with what actually happened.

Used in: 02 · Тихо в голові03 · Сміливо бути собою

Black-and-white thinking

An “all or nothing” thinking trap: either perfect or a failure, with nothing in between.

Used in: 02 · Тихо в голові03 · Сміливо бути собою

Burnout

Deep exhaustion after long-term stress: no energy, growing indifference, and work or caring for others stops bringing any joy. It is not laziness or weakness.

Used in: 04 · Знову з силами
C

Catastrophising

A thinking trap where a small event instantly turns into the worst-case scenario in your mind: “Everything is ruined.”

Used in: 02 · Тихо в голові03 · Сміливо бути собою

CBT — Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

A scientifically tested approach in psychotherapy. It teaches you to notice your thoughts, check whether they are fair, and gradually change habitual reactions and actions.

Used in: 02 · Тихо в голові03 · Сміливо бути собою

Channels of experience

The ways we remember and imagine: images (pictures), sounds, body sensations and inner self-talk.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Cognitive distortions (thinking traps)

Habitual mental shortcuts that distort reality: catastrophising, mind reading, black-and-white thinking, discounting the positive. Everyone has them; what matters is noticing them.

Used in: 02 · Тихо в голові03 · Сміливо бути собою
D

Defusion

A technique from Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: looking at a thought from the outside instead of merging with it. Instead of “I am a failure” — “I notice the thought that I am a failure”.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Dilts’ levels pyramid

A model for asking yourself questions: environment → behaviour → skills → beliefs → identity. It helps you find where a change is “stuck”. It is a framework for reflection, not a proven method.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Distress

Harmful, prolonged stress: tension lasts and rest never comes, so the body stays tense. If it goes on for months, it can turn into burnout.

Used in: 04 · Знову з силами
F

Fact vs interpretation

A fact is what a camera would record. An interpretation is what we think about it. Feelings often come from the interpretation.

Used in: 02 · Тихо в голові03 · Сміливо бути собою

Fear ladder

A plan of small steps from the easiest to the hardest situation you avoid. Step by step, anxiety decreases. Only for situations that are actually safe.

Used in: 02 · Тихо в голові
G

Grounding 5-4-3-2-1

An exercise that brings attention back to the here and now: name 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you can touch, 2 smells and 1 taste.

Used in: 01 · Тиша всередині
I

“If — then” plan

Deciding in advance: “If X happens, I will do Y.” This simple plan noticeably increases the chance that you actually follow through.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Inner critic

A habitual harsh, belittling way of talking to yourself. It often started as an attempt to protect you from mistakes or rejection.

Used in: 02 · Тихо в голові03 · Сміливо бути собою
M

Map of perception

An image used in psychotherapy: everyone sees an event through their own filters — experience, beliefs, values. So each person has their own “map” of the same event, and the map can be refined.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Mental rehearsal

Playing a difficult event through in your mind in advance — how it will go and what you will do. Athletes use it before competitions.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Mind reading

A thinking trap where we are sure we know what others think — usually something bad about us: “He didn’t reply — he must be offended.”

Used in: 02 · Тихо в голові03 · Сміливо бути собою

Mindfulness

The ability to be here and now, noticing your thoughts and sensations without judging yourself or escaping into the past or future.

Used in: 02 · Тихо в голові
P

Panic attack

A sudden strong wave of fear with intense body sensations: racing heart, shortness of breath, dizziness. Very unpleasant but not life-threatening: it usually peaks within 5–10 minutes and passes within 20–30 minutes. The first time, it is worth seeing a doctor.

Used in: 01 · Тиша всередині

Panic cycle

How panic feeds itself: a body sensation → a frightening thought about it → more anxiety → stronger sensations.

Used in: 01 · Тиша всередині

Perfectionism

Demanding that everything be done perfectly and treating any mistake as failure. A common road to burnout.

Used in: 04 · Знову з силами

Personal boundaries

What is and isn’t acceptable for you — in time, words, touch and tasks. Holding boundaries means speaking about them calmly.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою04 · Знову з силами

Positive psychotherapy

A direction in psychotherapy that builds on a person’s strengths. Its “four areas” model helps you see the balance: body and health, work, relationships, meaning and dreams.

Used in: 04 · Знову з силами

“Power pose”

The popular idea that standing wide with hands on hips for two minutes creates confidence. Repeated studies did not confirm it: posture may help you collect yourself a little, but it is not a confidence switch.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою
R

RAIN practice

Four steps to meet a difficult feeling without drowning in it: Recognize, Allow, Investigate, Nurture. Developed by psychologist Tara Brach.

Used in: 04 · Знову з силами

Resource state

A state in which you feel calm, collected and like yourself. You can learn to return to it through memories, your body and breathing.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою04 · Знову з силами
S

Self-compassion

Treating yourself in a hard moment the way you would treat someone close: with kindness, remembering that “this happens to everyone”, and without self-attack. It is not laziness — it more often helps you improve.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Self-distancing

Looking at a situation from the side, as if from far away or through someone else’s eyes. A well-studied way to reduce the intensity of emotions.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Self-efficacy

Belief that you can cope with a specific task. It grows most from your own experience of small successes.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

Self-esteem and self-worth

Self-esteem goes up and down with successes and failures. Self-worth is a deeper sense that you matter as a person, regardless of results.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою

SMART

A way to phrase a goal so it can actually be reached: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.

Used in: 03 · Сміливо бути собою
T

TOTE loop

Short for Test — Operate — Test — Exit. It shows movement towards a goal in loops: if it did not work, it is not a failure but the next loop.
